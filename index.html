        Questions people ask
        
  
        
    
        Do I have to start in January?
        No. Planner, Unscroll and Lovable calendars run for 12 months from the month you choose. The photo sheet shows one full year, January to December.
        
    
        How do my 5 dates get added?
        Send them on WhatsApp. We place them on our design, send you a preview, and print only after you approve it.
        
    
        How long does delivery take?
        Usually 3 to 4 days to design and print, then 3 to 6 days of shipping within India. Order by 20 Dec for New Year delivery.
        
    
        How do I pay?
        Once you confirm your order on WhatsApp, we share UPI and bank details. Design work starts after payment.
        
    
        Can you send it straight to the person?
        Yes. Give us their address. We leave out the invoice and price, and print your message on the card.
        
    
        What if something is printed wrong?
        Personalised items can't be returned, but if we make a printing mistake, we reprint it free.
        
    
        Will the chocolates survive the heat?
        In hot months we pack them with cooling sheets, or suggest a swap if your city is very hot.
